B7 Radio Player is a lightweight, open-source internet radio player for Android powered by Media3.

Features:
- Stream MP3, AAC, and OGG internet radio stations (including .m3u and .pls links)
- Browse and search stations by name, country, and genre via Radio Browser
- Folding Stream Capture panel (Record, Pause, Stop) with auto-split per song and automatic MP3 ID3 tags
- Recorded tab with seekable in-app replay and multi-select delete
- Configurable capture destination (Music, Downloads, or custom folder)
- Customizable accent color presets and interactive Color Wheel picker, plus Light/Dark themes
- Built-in equalizer (Bass, Mid, Treble) and Sleep Timer
- Manage favorites, station details, dead-station scanner, and import/export playlists (XML/M3U/PLS)
- Background playback with lock screen and Android Auto media controls
