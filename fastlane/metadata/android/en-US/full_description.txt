B7 Radio Player is a lightweight, open-source internet radio player for Android powered by Media3.

Features:
- Stream MP3 and AAC internet radio stations (including .m3u and .pls links)
- Search and add stations from Radio Browser
- Built-in equalizer with Bass, Mid, and Treble controls
- Manage favorites, station details, and custom station lists
- Import and export station playlists (XML)
- Background playback with lock screen and Android Auto media controls
